Momoka bundled runtime notices

The two DLLs in this directory are copied next to Momoka.exe on every build and
publish. The license text for each ships in the publish root:

  LICENSE                  Momoka itself (MIT)
  LICENSE.LGPL-2.1.txt     libmpv-2.dll, the mpv code
  LICENSE.LGPL-3.0.txt     libmpv-2.dll, the FFmpeg it links statically
  LICENSE.GPL-3.0.txt      referenced by the LGPL-3.0 text above
  LICENSE.Apache-2.0.txt   vulkan-1.dll

The three LGPL/GPL files are byte-identical copies of the canonical ones under
assets/shaders, where the shader sources point at them. tools/verify-publish.ps1
compares the two locations, so they cannot drift apart.

  Why this is the LGPL build, not the GPL one. The binary reports its own
  configure line at startup. The "--vo=help" style listings are gone with
  -Dcplayer=false, so read it through the libmpv log interface:

    -Dgpl=false -Ddvdnav=disabled -Drubberband=disabled -Dopenal=disabled
    -Dcplayer=false -Dlibmpv=true ...

  Its enabled-features line carries no gpl, direct3d, dvdnav or rubberband, and
  the strings libx264, libx265, libdvdcss and dvdread appear zero times in the
  file (the previous GPL build had 5, 10, 3 and 6 occurrences).

  Consequence: LGPL allows linking without the copyleft reaching the application,
  so Momoka is distributed under its own license (MIT, see LICENSE). The DLL is
  still an LGPL work -- its license texts travel with it, and its corresponding
  source is at the upstream addresses above. Until 2026-10-14 the bundled DLL was
  a GPL build, which forced the whole application to GPL-2.0-or-later; that
  history and the re-check procedure are in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md and in the
  license audit under docs/.

  Unmodified: shipped exactly as upstream builds it.
